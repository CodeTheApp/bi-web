# Estados de luz dos produtos

Em 12/11/2026, as quatro imagens editoriais existentes foram editadas com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen` para desligar somente a luz emitida pela peça, preservando enquadramento, produto e iluminação ambiente.

As capas com luz desligada são a primeira mídia da variante; as versões ligadas são a segunda. Os pares usam o mesmo `variantId`. A troca no hover só ocorre entre mídias visíveis do mesmo acabamento. As versões ligadas continuam disponíveis na galeria em telas de toque.

## Marie rosé

- Desligada: [marie-rose-editorial-off.webp](../public/img/products/marie-rose-editorial-off.webp).
- Ligada: [marie-rose-editorial.webp](../public/img/products/marie-rose-editorial.webp).

### Prompt da edição

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit this exact image to make a matching LIGHT-OFF version for an e-commerce hover comparison. Turn OFF the internal LED in both transparent acrylic wings of this Marie pendant. Remove only the self-emitted warm glow and warm LED sparkle/illumination inside the wings and on the metal body. Keep transparent embossed wing texture, sunlight reflections and rose-gold finish exactly intact.
CRITICAL: This is the SAME photograph, not a new scene. Preserve camera, perspective, image dimensions, crop, exact product position/scale, all object edges and geometry, materials, colors, wall texture, architecture, limestone console, background shadows, sun patches and overall ambient daylight exposure pixel-for-pixel as closely as possible. Do NOT darken or recolor the whole image. Only the lamp's electrically emitted light is switched off. The warm daylight room stays equally bright. No new objects, no text, no added logos, no watermark. Return the original portrait 4:5 composition.
```
